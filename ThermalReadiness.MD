```
Application Readiness
Liquid Metal TIM (ThermalGrizzly Conductonaut)
Supplied: Syringe/tube form.
Application: Directly applied to CPU/GPU dies.
Ready‑to‑apply, but requires careful spreading and insulation (not die‑cut).

Phase‑Change Pads (e.g., Honeywell PTM7950, Panasonic Soft‑PCM)
Supplied: Sheets or pre‑cut pads.
Application: Can be die‑cut to fit VRMs, memory modules, SSD controllers.
Often require die‑cutting for precise fit, though some vendors supply pre‑shaped pads.

Nano Gap Fillers (e.g., silicone‑based thermal gap fillers)
Supplied: Sheets, rolls, or dispensable putty.
Application: Fill irregular gaps between components and heatsinks.
Ready‑to‑apply in putty form; die‑cut if sheet‑based.

Graphite Thermal Sheets (e.g., Panasonic PGS, Fujipoly)
Supplied: Large sheets.
Application: Spread heat across DIMMs, SSDs, or chassis.
Require die‑cutting to match component outlines.

Heat Spreading Solutions (Graphene vapor chamber, copper plates)
Supplied: Custom‑engineered assemblies.
Application: Integrated into chassis or cooling stack.
Always custom‑cut/engineered, not off‑the‑shelf.

Supplied - die‑cut pads/sheets to exact dimensions for VRMs, MOSFETs, SSD controllers, and memory stacks, while TIMs and fillers are applied directly.
```
